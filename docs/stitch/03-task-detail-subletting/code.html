The Tenant shall not assign, sublet, transfer, or part with the possession of the Demised Premises or any part thereof without the prior written consent of the Landlord, which consent may be granted at the Landlord's sole and absolute discretion for up to 50% of the gross floor areaprovided that for any JTC industrial property, such subletting shall strictly not exceed 30% of the gross floor area and shall be subject to mandatory lodgment of statutory declaration of prevailing market rental terms with the Landlord prior to formal execution of the sublease. Any sublease entered into in contravention of this clause shall be void ab initio.

error_outline

Why this was flagged

Parliament confirmed a reduction in permitted subletting limits from 50% to 30% for JTC industrial leasehold covenants, alongside mandatory statutory declarations. Clause 7.2 in its current version permits terms inconsistent with the updated JTC statutory framework, exposing the client to potential breach of head-lease terms.
